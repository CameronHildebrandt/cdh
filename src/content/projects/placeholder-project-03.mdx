An interface for exploring simulated biometric signal data in a compact, dark-first dashboard. The screen presents stacked live-style traces and clear controls for switching among EEG, accelerometer (ACC), and photoplethysmography (PPG) views.

The visual direction keeps the display focused: a NeurAlbertaTech wordmark, a concise “EEG | Simulated Data” status label, and high-contrast signal lines carry the experience without competing UI.
